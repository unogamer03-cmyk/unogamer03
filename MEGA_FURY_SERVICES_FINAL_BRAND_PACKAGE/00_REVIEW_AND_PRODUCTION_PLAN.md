## 2. Inconsistencies found
**Logo**
1. **There are two different MF monograms.** In Badge A the MF is flat white, and a long green blade sits *under* the letters, sweeping from lower left to the right. In Promos B/C the MF is metallic silver. The green swoosh arcs *over the top of the F*, and green "road" strokes run inside the M. The letter shapes, the joins, and the swoosh geometry all differ. These are two separate logos, not two versions of one logo.
2. **The promo MF is not consistent with itself.** The MF on the truck trailer, the laptop, the phone, and the header in B/C is drawn slightly differently each time. That is typical of AI image generation.
3. **The wordmark changes.** In A, MEGA FURY is all white. In B/C, MEGA is white and FURY is green, set in italic.
4. **Fonts don't match the brief.** The references use a wide geometric sans, close to Montserrat ExtraBold. The brief specifies Rajdhani Bold, which is narrower and squarer, so the wordmark will look different if it is re-set in Rajdhani. Recommendation: keep the MEGA FURY lettering as **artwork** taken from the approved master, and use Rajdhani only for headlines.

**Color**
5. The references use several greens: ring ≈ #2AFB3F, swoosh ≈ #2CF715 with a gradient, rules ≈ #66F72D. The brief's **Fury Green #39F45A** is close. The plan is to standardize every green to #39F45A and drop the gradients.

**Copy and claims (owner must confirm)**
6. "A **Modem** TMS" in B is a typo for "Modern".
7. **"Start Your Free Trial"** appears in B and C, and the phone mockup has a "Free trial" tab. This is unverified, so it will not be used until the owner confirms that a free trial exists.
8. Panel B features (Track Operations, Control Costs, Boost Profitability, Work with Multiple Carriers, and so on) are unverified, so each one is flagged for owner approval.
9. The C dashboard mockup is a *master-admin* screen showing $0.00 revenue and 0 subscribers. That is not suitable for marketing and should be replaced with a neutral UI mockup or a real screenshot from the owner.
10. B/C target audiences (Carriers / Dispatchers / Brokers), while the brief lists services (Transportation, Dispatching, Travel Planning, TMS). The service list for the cover must be confirmed.
11. The people and truck photos in B/C are AI-generated and show minor artifacts. For final posts we need licensed stock photos or the owner's own photos, or the owner needs to approve AI imagery.

## 3. Master Logo: the honest status
- A clean, exact logo **cannot be extracted** from these rasters, because there is no source file.
- A **vector reconstruction** of Badge A was made by auto-tracing the badge pixels: `01_MASTER_LOGO/_DRAFT_FOR_APPROVAL/`. Side by side with the original it is very close (see the COMPARE png). It is still a **reconstruction, not the original file**. Edges are smoothed, and all colors are set to the brief's palette.
- Recommendation: **Badge A is the Master.** It is the only clean, logo-only reference, and its MF and swoosh are a single, consistent drawing. The promo MF changes between appearances.
- If the owner has the original .ai, .svg, .eps or .pdf from whoever made the logo, send it. That file replaces this reconstruction.
